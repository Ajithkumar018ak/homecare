Patient Privacy Policy

How AuraCare protects patient diagnostics, bio-telemetry reports, and invoice information in compliance with HIPAA and ISO guidelines.

1. Data Telemetry Collection

We collect patient vital stats streaming from physical cuffs (blood pressure, oxygen levels, heart rhythms) solely to analyze health metrics and authorize dispatcher ambulance protocols when medical alerts fire.

2. Medical Record Confidentiality

Clinical logs, test results, and diagnostic histories are stored on servers protected by AES-256 encryption. Only authorized supervising physicians and designated roster nurses have access clearance keys.

3. Telehealth Streaming Rules

Video consultation rooms and chat histories between patient and clinicians are end-to-end encrypted under HIPAA compliance models. No third-party data tracking is permitted.
