Frequently Asked Questions

How is telemetry vitals data synchronized? +
Vitals (heart rate, blood pressure, oxygen levels) are synced automatically every 6 hours via your wearable AuraCare hub device or blood pressure cuff accessory. You can also manual log entries using the Quick Actions menu.
How do I request a medication refill? +
When active prescriptions fall below 15 days of remaining tablets, a Refill Alert card triggers. Clicking the refill button instantly dispatches a request code to your prescribing clinical coordinator.
How are insurance claims processed? +
Claims are automatically generated upon publication of diagnostic reports or consult completion. They are electronically dispatched directly to BlueShield or UnitedHealth for coverage processing.
