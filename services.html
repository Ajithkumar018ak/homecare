Our Coordination Workflow

We guide patients through a structured clinical pipeline, from intake to active monitoring.

01

Clinical Intake

Submit diagnosis reports and clinical records. Our triaging director verifies requirements.

02

Bedside Assessment

A board-certified specialist conducts an on-site visit to inspect vitals and calibrate equipment.

03

Roster Deployment

Designated registered nurses are assigned to the household under a customized calendar shift.

04

Active Telemetry Loop

Blood oxygen, ECG trends, and heart metrics are continually analyzed by the command room.
