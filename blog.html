Preventive Guide

Preventive Healthcare & Wellness Checklist

Preventive home evaluations form the bedrock of safe recoveries. By implementing checks early, you bypass emergency visits.

  • Vitals Logging: Track blood pressure and pulse twice daily at uniform coordinates.
  • Dietary Balance: Structure low-sodium meals to prevent cardiac fluid weight shifts.
  • Mobility Clears: Eliminate rugs, clutter, and low-lighting areas in main hallways.

Daily Doctor Recommendations

  • Cardiology Note: Patients recovering from stents must avoid lifting weight over 10 lbs during the first 14 days post-discharge.
  • Endocrinology Note: Calibrate blood glucose sensors before breakfast to ensure accurate baseline insulin checks.
