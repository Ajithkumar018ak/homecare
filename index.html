Common Inquiries

Frequently Asked Questions

Learn more about our home clinical coordination and telehealth telemetry solutions.

What regions do AuraCare mobile teams cover?

Our active clinical coordination desks serve the Greater New York Metropolitan area, central London districts, Singapore clinics, and the Toronto metro region. We dispatch registered nurses and equipment vans directly from our regional hubs.

Is home-based clinical ICU care covered by health insurance?

Yes, AuraCare is an in-network provider with premium insurance plans including Aetna, Blue Cross Alliance, and Cigna. Home ICU setups and active telehealth monitoring require a physician referral and insurance pre-authorization.

How are telemetry alerts and diagnostics handled at home?

Every active bed is equipped with a digital telemetry hub that monitors vitals like blood pressure, oxygen saturation, and heart rate. If any metric crosses safe thresholds, our central Telehealth Control Room receives an automated alert and dispatches mobile units immediately.
